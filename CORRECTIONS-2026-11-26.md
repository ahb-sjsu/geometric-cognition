# Corrections, 2026-11-26

These corrections were made on 2026-11-26 at the author's direction after a review of the Geometric Series.

The repository holds no primary run records for the Measuring AGI benchmarks (`claims/LEDGER.md` says so). A benchmark value was therefore changed only where the rest of the volume, including Appendix B, Appendix C and `BOOK_PLAN.md`, agrees against a single outlying passage, or where the value can be recomputed from numbers the volume prints. Every other conflict is listed under Unresolved and the text was left as it was.
